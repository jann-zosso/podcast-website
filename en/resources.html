English-language resources on recovery from Long Covid and neuroplastic symptoms

“Knowledge is the first step towards recovery.”

Dr Howard Schubiner

A curated selection of English-language resources featuring recovery stories and the neuroplastic model of Long Covid, chronic pain and other persistent symptoms. The material draws on modern pain science and neurobiology.

Recovery did not happen overnight. Step by step, trust in the body returned, symptoms became less threatening, and everyday activities gradually became possible again. The story traces Jann's path from severe Long Covid and ME/CFS-like symptoms back to an active life, and the change in understanding that became central to his recovery.Recovery did not happen overnight. Documentaries & podcasts

Pain Brain
Documentary, 2023

The film follows a study of Pain Reprocessing Therapy for chronic back pain and uses personal stories to explore the potential of this neuroplastic treatment approach.

This Might Hurt
Documentary, 2021

An introduction to Dr Howard Schubiner's work and to a different understanding of chronic pain and persistent symptoms.


Tell Me About Your Pain
Podcast series, 2021

Alan Gordon and Alon Ziv respond to people living with chronic pain and explain neuroscience-based techniques aimed at recovery.

Changing Your Mind: Chronic Pain and The Brain
Short documentary, 2022

Leading voices from neuroscience, medicine and psychology explain how the brain and neuroplasticity can open new approaches to chronic pain beyond medication and surgery.
