The team behind SO LONG COVID

We would like to thank everyone who made this podcast adventure possible: our incredible team, who have put so much dedication into SO LONG COVID. Our financial supporters, who believe in us and in the project. Our families and friends, who encourage us, cheer us on and celebrate with us. All our sources of inspiration: Hysterical, Oklou, Sauna-Gus, cycling in Copenhagen and much more. And above all, all our interviewees, who so generously shared their stories and expertise with us — without all of you, this project would not have been possible!
